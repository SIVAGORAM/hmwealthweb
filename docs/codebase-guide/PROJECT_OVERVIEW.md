# HM Wealth — Project Overview

## 1. What Is This Website?
**HM Wealth** (`https://hmwealth.in`) is a production-grade, compliance-first marketing and lead-generation website for an **AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor** and **IRDAI-licensed insurance partner** based in India.

- **Founder:** Hari Sarvotham A
- **AMFI Registration:** ARN-341412 (Mutual Fund Distributor & SIF Distributor)
- **Target Audience:** Indian retail investors, families, salaried professionals, business owners, and NRIs (Non-Resident Indians) seeking guidance on mutual funds, SIPs, life & health insurance, and sovereign/digital gold.

## 2. Business & Regulatory Model
In India, the financial regulatory framework (SEBI, AMFI, IRDAI) maintains a strict legal distinction between **Investment Advisers (RIA)** and **Mutual Fund Distributors (MFD)**:
1. **Commission Model:** HM Wealth distributes mutual fund schemes and insurance policies, earning commissions directly from Asset Management Companies (AMCs) and insurance providers. It does **not** charge direct advisory fees to retail investors.
2. **Strict Compliance Guardrails (AMFI / SEBI Regulations):**
   - **Prohibited Words:** The website and its developers must **NEVER** use words such as: *advisor/adviser, advisory, consultant, financial planner/planning, wealth manager/management, wealth creator, "best", "No. 1", "guaranteed returns"*.
   - **Required Disclosures:** Every page contains or links to mandatory statutory warnings ("Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks..."), ARN numbers, EUIN, IRDAI licensing details, and return illustration caps (capped at 13% p.a. in accordance with AMFI illustration norms).

## 3. Core Value Proposition & User Journeys
The website is structured to build trust with conservative, family-oriented investors:
1. **Education & Demystification:** Explains complex concepts (SIP vs Lumpsum, ELSS lock-in, Riskometer, Cashless health claims) in plain, reassuring English.
2. **Interactive Numbers Discovery:** Users interact with real-time financial calculators (SIP, Lumpsum, Step-up SIP, SWP, Loan EMI, Goal SIP) to plan retirement, children's education, or home down-payments.
3. **High-Conversion Contact Flow:** Instead of a complex login or generic database form, visitors request a callback via an automated, pre-formatted **WhatsApp message** or direct phone call.
